# CS-255-DriverPass Project Reflection
Project Summary

The DriverPass project was about designing a system for a company that helps people get ready for their driving tests. The client wanted a system where customers could take practice tests online and schedule driving lessons. The system also needed to let employees manage appointments and help the business keep everything organized.

What I Did Well

I think I did well with creating my diagrams and putting the information together in my system design document. I worked on showing how the system would work and how customers and employees would use it. This project helped me understand how important it is to have clear requirements and a plan before a system is built.

What I Would Improve

If I could go back and change one part of my work, I would spend more time reviewing my diagrams and making sure everything was clear. I would check that the diagrams matched the written requirements and that someone who had not worked on the project could understand them without having to guess what something meant.

Understanding the User's Needs

I used the information from the client to figure out what features the DriverPass system needed. This included scheduling driving lessons, checking if a driver and vehicle were available, and allowing customers to take practice tests. I think understanding the user's needs is important because the system needs to work for the people who will actually use it. If the requirements are not understood correctly, the finished system might not do what the customer needs it to do.

My Approach to System Design

When designing a system, I would start by figuring out what the client needs and what problems the system is supposed to solve. Then I would organize the requirements and use diagrams to show how everything should work. I would also go back and review my work to make sure it makes sense and ask questions if I was unsure about something. In the future, I can use what I learned in this class to help me plan systems, communicate ideas, and make sure the design meets the user's needs before development starts.
